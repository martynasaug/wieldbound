3D assets used by the Three.js client (Phase 47 onward). All CC0 (public
domain equivalent); no attribution is legally required, but provenance is
recorded here so every asset stays traceable — same policy as the 2D art in
../assets/ASSET_CREDITS.txt.

Warrior_Sword.fbx      https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-rpg-characters
Ranger_Bow.fbx         https://quaternius.com/  (CC0 1.0 Universal)
Ranger_Arrow.fbx
Rogue_Dagger.fbx       6 rigged, animated, textured fantasy characters. The
Wizard_Staff.fbx       warrior carries 14 animation clips: Idle, Idle2,
Cleric_Staff.fbx       Idle_Weapon, Idle_Attacking, Walk, Run, Run_Weapon,
                       Sword_Attack, Sword_AttackFast, RecieveHit,
                       RecieveHit_Attacking, Roll, PickUp, Death.

                       The rig ships a dedicated `WeaponR` socket bone with
                       the weapon already parented to it. That is what makes
                       "your class is whatever you are holding" a mesh swap
                       on one bone, with every animation carrying it for free.

TEXTURES

../textures/*.png      Shipped with the packs above. Material names mostly
                       match file names; the loader resolves exact, then
                       `<name>_Texture`, then a prefix match, and falls back
                       to a flat colour (see assets.ts).

NOTE ON flipY

Textures keep three.js's default flipY (true). The usual advice for FBX is to
force it false — doing that here put every UV island on the wrong part of the
atlas and rendered the warrior's steel plate as the brown leather sitting
directly above it in the sheet.
